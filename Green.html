Green Eyes

Green eyes are the considered the rarest natural eye color, found in only about 2% of the world’s population. A fun fact is that the human iris does not actually contain green pigment, insider it's color is a unique optical anomaly caused by low melanin levels and light scattering. This causes green eyes to often appear to shift shades depending on your lighting, environment, and clothing.
